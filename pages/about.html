Our Story

A Palace for the senses

Maison Royale was conceived as a single question: what if a shopping destination could feel like the greatest department store ever imagined — and a private gallery at the same time?

  • Designed in harmony

    Travertine, smoked oak and bronze across 96,000 m², crowned by a 96-metre glass atrium.

  • Curated by experts

    Thirty boutiques, twelve tables and eight experiences — each one chosen by a dedicated house.

  • Quietly considerate

    Concierge on every floor, private salons and one promise: the maison always anticipates.

Privacy Policy

Maison Royale collects only the information you choose to share — through forms, the newsletter and loyalty programmes — to personalise your experience and improve the maison. Your data is never sold. You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by writing to privacy@maisonroyale.com.

Terms of Service

Entry to the maison implies acceptance of our house rules: courteous conduct, no unauthorised photography in private suites, and supervision of minors. Offers are subject to availability and expire on their stated dates. The Royale Card remains the property of Maison Royale.
